End-to-End Workflow

How StudySync Operates

A streamlined pipeline from document upload to actionable academic insights.

01

Upload Files

Drop in PDF or TXT notes, or upload your course syllabus alongside previous-year question papers.

02

Extract Text

PyMuPDF extracts clean text page-by-page while preserving structural integrity and source references.

03

Process & Vectorize

TF-IDF transforms text into high-dimensional vector representations ready for scikit-learn models.

04

Review & Explore

Inspect predicted subjects, review mapped questions against syllabus topics, and examine visual trends.
