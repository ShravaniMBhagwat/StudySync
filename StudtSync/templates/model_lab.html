B. Dimensionality reduction: TruncatedSVD

TF-IDF text matrices are sparse. TruncatedSVD works directly with sparse input and does not center it. PCA normally centers features, which can turn a large sparse matrix dense and consume substantial memory. Unlike PCA, TruncatedSVD on uncentered data is commonly used as latent semantic analysis.
