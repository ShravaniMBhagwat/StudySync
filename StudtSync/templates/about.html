Overview & Architecture

About StudySync

A modern, machine-learning-powered study material organiser and previous-year question paper analyser.

💡

What StudySync Does

StudySync provides a structured, intelligent environment for computer science students to organize class notes, extract meaningful concepts, and analyze examination question papers with machine-learning assistance.

It combines transparent lexical matching, supervised classification, and unsupervised topic clustering into an intuitive web workflow.

🔒

Privacy & Data Safety

Your uploaded documents are processed securely in temporary storage and removed immediately after text extraction. In-memory data structures expire automatically, ensuring your study materials remain private.

No documents are retained, executed, or permanently stored in databases.

⚙️

Extraction & Processing Engine

StudySync utilizes PyMuPDF for high-fidelity text extraction across multi-page PDF documents, alongside native Python readers for plain text files.

Extracted texts are tokenized, normalized, and converted into mathematical representations using TF-IDF vectors.

🔬

Learning Models & Diagnostics

Classifiers include Multinomial Naive Bayes and Linear Support Vector Machines (SVM) evaluated via 5-fold stratified cross-validation.

Question paper analysis applies Cosine Similarity against syllabus topics, while the Model Lab allows exploration of Chi-Squared feature selection, TruncatedSVD reduction, and KMeans clustering.
